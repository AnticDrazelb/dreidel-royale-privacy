How to Play
Antes rise as the game goes on · hold to charge, release to spin
נNUNnisht — nothing happens.
גGIMELgants — sweep the whole pot.
הHEIhalb — take half the pot.
שSHINshtel — put one in the pot.
The letters spell Nes Gadol Haya Sham — "a great miracle happened there."
Play actions come from the Yiddish: nisht / gants / halb / shtel. Run out of gelt and you're out — last player holding gelt wins.
